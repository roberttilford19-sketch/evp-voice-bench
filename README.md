# EVP Voice Bench

A listening bench for recordings — record or import audio, view it as a
waveform, play it back slowly, reversed, filtered, or looped on one small
stretch at a time, and write down what you hear in your own words before
anyone or anything suggests a wording to you.

**Live app:** https://roberttilford19-sketch.github.io/evp-voice-bench/

## Privacy — plainly

- No accounts. No sign-in. No data collection. No analytics — none was
  added on this shelf, and the app itself makes no network calls.
- Your recordings and your notes live only on your own device, in the
  app's on-device storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are
  never in the shelf owner's keeping. The only way a recording leaves
  your device is if you deliberately export and share it yourself.
- The original recording is never altered. Every effect works on a copy
  or at playback.
- The app never transcribes, never captions, and never suggests words.
  What you hear is written down by you, first.
- The only thing this shelf shows its owner is anonymous counts of
  visits and downloads, of the kind any web host keeps. Nothing about
  you, your recordings, or your notes can be seen by the owner — if your
  own device is lost or compromised, that keeping is yours, the same as
  any file on your phone.

## Honest limits

This is a listening tool, not a scientific instrument. It cannot prove
where a voice came from. What a faint voice means is the listener's
judgement, not the app's.
